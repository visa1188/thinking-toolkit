Foundational Learning

Foundational Learning builds essential concepts that children use later in thinking, problem-solving, and everyday life. These modules are organized into progressive levels.

Level 1 – Recognition Foundations

  • Colors
  • Shapes
  • Sizes
  • Numbers
  • Animals
  • Fruits
  • Vegetables
  • Vehicles
  • Basic Objects

Level 2 – Concept Foundations

  • Opposites
  • Categories
  • Matching by Function
  • Counting Groups
  • Comparing Quantities

Level 3 – Relational Foundations

  • Position
  • Direction
  • Sequence
  • Before and After
  • Simple Cause & Effect
