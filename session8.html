Session 8 – Spatial Thinking

In this session, children practice understanding position and orientation. They learn to describe where objects are placed.

Session Goal: Help your child understand spatial relationships like above, below, inside, and outside.

Activity 1

●
■
For parents:

Ask: Which shape is above? Which is below?

What to notice: Vertical position

Activity 2

●
■
For parents:

Ask: Which shape is inside the box? Which is outside?

What to notice: Containment relationship

Activity 3

▲
▲
For parents:

Ask: Do these look the same or different?

If unsure, say: Is one turned upside down?

What to notice: Orientation

You have completed Session 8. Spatial thinking supports reading, writing, and mathematical understanding.
