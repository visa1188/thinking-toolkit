Session 9 – Logical Elimination

In this session, children practice eliminating objects that do not follow a rule. They learn to reason by removing what does not fit.

Session Goal: Help your child remove objects that do not match the rule.

Activity 1

●
■
●
●
For parents:

Ask: Which one does not follow the rule?

If unsure, say: Are most of them the same color?

Rule to notice: Color

Activity 2

■
■
■
●
For parents:

Ask: Which one does not belong?

If unsure, say: Are most of them the same shape?

Rule to notice: Shape

Activity 3

●
●
●
●
For parents:

Ask: Which one is different in size?

If unsure, say: Do most of them look the same size?

Rule to notice: Size

You have completed Session 9. Logical elimination builds structured reasoning and early problem-solving skills.
