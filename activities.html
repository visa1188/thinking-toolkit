Activities

Before starting
Some activities use basic ideas like colors or shapes. If your child hasn’t seen these before, that’s okay — help them once and continue. The goal is noticing patterns, not knowing names.

The first one or two activities can be treated as a warm-up.

These activities help children practice noticing what is same and what is different. There is no fixed order, but starting from the top usually works well.
