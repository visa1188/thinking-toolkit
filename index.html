Thinking Toolkit for Kids (Age 4–6)

Disclaimer: This is an early version shared for feedback from parents.

This is a small collection of thinking activities for young children. There are no lessons, no right–wrong teaching, and no pressure.

Each activity trains your child to notice: what is same, what is different, and why.

Sit with your child. Read the question aloud. Let them answer in their own words.

👉 Start the sessions

How to use this

  • Sit with your child and read the question aloud.
  • Let your child answer in their own words or by pointing.
  • Ask “why?” if they are confident.
  • If they struggle, ask “what is same in these?”
  • There is no need to correct immediately.

One or two activities in one sitting is enough. Stop when your child loses interest.
