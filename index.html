Foundational Thinking for Kids

A structured program to build core thinking habits.

The program progresses through guided sessions that develop observation, reasoning, and flexible thinking.

Disclaimer: This is an early version shared for feedback from parents.

What This Program Builds

  • Observation skills
  • Classification ability
  • Pattern recognition
  • Comparison and reasoning
  • Clear explanation of thinking

Sit with your child. Ask the questions aloud. Encourage them to think and explain in their own words.

👉 Start the program

How to use this

  • Read each prompt aloud.
  • Allow your child to respond in their own words or by pointing.
  • Encourage explanation by asking “Why?” when they are confident.
  • If they struggle, gently ask what they notice.
  • Avoid rushing or correcting immediately — the goal is thinking, not speed.

One or two activities at a time is enough. End the session while your child is still engaged.
