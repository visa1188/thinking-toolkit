Session 7 – Matching & Pairing

In this session, children practice matching objects that belong together. They learn to recognize relationships between pairs.

Session Goal: Help your child identify objects that logically go together.

Activity 1

●
■
●
■
For parents:

Ask: Which two shapes match each other?

What to notice: Same shapes form pairs.

Activity 2

●
●
■
■
For parents:

Ask: Can you match objects of the same color?

What to notice: Same colors form pairs.

Activity 3

🔒
🔑
🧦
🧦
For parents:

Ask: Which objects belong together?

What to notice: Some objects naturally form pairs.
