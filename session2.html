Session 2 – Noticing Difference

In this session, children practice identifying what is different among similar objects. The goal is to notice one object that does not follow the shared feature.

Session Goal: Help your child identify what does not belong in a group.

Activity 1

●
●
●
●
For parents:

Ask: Which one is different?

If unsure, say: Do most of them look the same?

What to notice: Color

Activity 2

●
●
●
■
For parents:

Ask: Which one does not belong?

If unsure, say: Are most of these the same shape?

What to notice: Shape

Activity 3

●
●
●
●
For parents:

Ask: Which one looks different in size?

If unsure, say: Do three of them look the same size?

What to notice: Size

You have completed Session 2. Recognizing differences strengthens observation and comparison skills.
