Session 1 – Noticing Sameness

In this session, children practice seeing what is the same between different objects. The goal is to slow down and notice shared features.

Session Goal: Help your child notice shared features between objects before focusing on differences.

Activity 1

●
■
▲
For parents:

Ask: What is same in these?

If your child is unsure, say: Look carefully. Do they share something?

What to notice: Color

Activity 2

●
●
●
For parents:

Ask: What is same in these?

If your child is unsure, say: Are they the same shape?

What to notice: Shape

Activity 3

🐶
🐱
🐭
For parents:

Ask: What is same in these?

If your child is unsure, say: Are they about the same size?

What to notice: Size

You have completed Session 1. Children who can notice sameness build strong foundations for later learning.
