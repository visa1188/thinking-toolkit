Session 4 – Switching the Rule

In this session, children practice changing the rule used to group objects. The same objects can be organized in different ways.

Session Goal: Help your child understand that grouping depends on the rule chosen.

Activity 1

●
●
■
■
For parents:

Step 1: Group these by color.

Step 2: Now regroup them by shape.

What to notice: The grouping changes when the rule changes.

Activity 2

●
■
●
■
For parents:

Step 1: Group these by shape.

Step 2: Now regroup them by size.

What to notice: The same objects can fit different groups.

Activity 3

●
●
■
■
For parents:

Step 1: Ask your child to choose a rule and group them.

Step 2: Ask them to change the rule and regroup.

What to notice: Flexible thinking.

You have completed Session 4. Switching rules builds cognitive flexibility and adaptive thinking.
